Understanding

What is Cashback?

Cashback (rebate) is a partial refund of your trading costs: part of the spread you pay becomes a partner commission that Markets4You pays us — and we return up to 90% of that commission to you.

Example: you trade and pay a spread — e.g. $20 per lot. Markets4You shares part of it with us as partner commission, and up to 90% of that commission goes back into your own Markets4You account daily. Your spread never changes.

  • From 0.01 Lot — every order earns cashback, no minimum
  • Zero impact on your trading results or account
  • Transparent system, verifiable directly via Markets4You
  • Supports all accounts: Standard / PRO / Standard Cent
